5 · Theory and validity

What does B-spline inversion do?

Represent n(λ) and k(λ) as sums of overlapping smooth basis functions. The Python engine fits their coefficients to all selected R/T spectra using exact coherent-film Fresnel equations and a curvature penalty. This enforces smoothness, not Kramers–Kronig causality.

What is the benefit of multiple spectra?

Thin and thick films and different substrates produce different optical sensitivities. Joint inversion requires one shared n(λ), k(λ) to explain all selected configurations. This is valid only when the films have identical intrinsic optical properties.

How should I interpret the optimizer comparison?

Compare recovered n/k errors (available for synthetic reference spectra), forward spectral RMSE, convergence, and runtime. A small spectral residual does not prove a unique solution. HHO is a stochastic alternative for difficult multimodal problems; it is not automatically superior.

Current scientific limitations

Four built-in cases use synthetic R6G spectra and a semi-infinite substrate. On a hosted deployment, computations and selected example settings are sent to the hosting server; do not submit confidential experimental data.
